# One-Day Meal Plan for Marathon Training Athlete

Here is a one-day meal plan for an athlete training for a marathon with high-endurance requirements:

* **Calorie Target:** Approximately 3200 calories
* **Macronutrient Breakdown:** Protein (130g), Fat (85g), Carbs (420g)

## Meal Plan:

* **Breakfast (6:30 AM):** Oatmeal (100g dry weight) with banana (150g), honey (20g), and whole milk (250g). Side of whole wheat toast (60g) with almond butter (20g).
    * Protein: 18g, Fat: 12g, Carbs: 95g

* **Pre-Workout Snack (8:30 AM - 1 hour before training):** White bread (80g) with jam (30g) and a sports drink (500ml containing 30g carbs).
    * Protein: 6g, Fat: 2g, Carbs: 80g

* **Lunch (1:00 PM):** Grilled chicken breast (150g) with brown rice (150g cooked) and steamed carrots (150g). Olive oil drizzle (15ml).
    * Protein: 45g, Fat: 18g, Carbs: 75g

* **Post-Workout Snack (3:30 PM):** Chocolate milk (400ml) with granola bar (50g).
    * Protein: 16g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 85g

* **Afternoon Snack (5:00 PM):** Orange (200g) with rice cakes (50g) and peanut butter (15g).
    * Protein: 8g, Fat: 8g, Carbs: 50g

* **Dinner (7:00 PM):** Lean ground turkey (160g) with pasta (120g dry weight) in tomato sauce (150g) and olive oil (10ml). Side salad with mixed greens (100g).
    * Protein: 35g, Fat: 20g, Carbs: 85g

* **Evening Hydration (Before bed):** Water (500ml) with electrolyte powder and a banana (120g).
    * Protein: 2g, Fat: 0g, Carbs: 30g

**Hydration Strategy:** Drink 500-750ml water with each meal, plus 150-250ml every 15-20 minutes during training sessions. This meal plan emphasizes carbohydrate loading for sustained energy, adequate protein for muscle recovery, and strategic timing around workouts for optimal performance.
